The Disclosure of Real Estate Brokerage Relationships reminds consumers that they have a choice: to be represented by a brokerage firm or to represent themselves. Until the consumer decides to enter into a representation agreement, she will not be entitled to advocacy or advice on price or terms, and only limited confidentiality.

Here are two useful statements for agents to remember:

Agents need to know what their clients need to know even before the clients know they need to know!
I cannot make that decision for you because I don't have to live with the consequences!
