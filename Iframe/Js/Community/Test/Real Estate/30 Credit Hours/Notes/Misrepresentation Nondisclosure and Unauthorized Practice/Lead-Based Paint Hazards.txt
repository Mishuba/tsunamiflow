The Lead-Based Paint Hazard Reduction Act requires that sellers of homes built before 1978 disclose the presence of any known lead-based paint and to provide buyers with copies of any testing results. It also requires that the sellers and their agents provide the HUD booklet Protect Your Family from Lead in Your Home, which is currently available in English, Spanish, Vietnamese, Russian, Arabic, and Somali. Furthermore, the Act requires that the buyer be allowed a 10-day window to test the property, or another time frame agreed to by both buyer and seller, or the buyer may waive the right. The Act does not require any removal of lead-based paint. Click here for more information about lead disclosure.

Sellers must also acknowledge, if applicable, the presence of lead-based paint on a federally designed disclosure statement, which can be found here: https://www.hud.gov/sites/documents/DOC_12343.PDF

It is very important to remember that no one has to test for nor remove anything.

This disclosure form must be signed by both sellers (if more than one) and both buyers (if more than one) and the documents retained for three years.
