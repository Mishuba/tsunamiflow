Generally, everyone involved in the real estate transaction is committed to the successful transfer of real property. The goals of buyers, sellers, and their agents are more similar than not. It is especially helpful when buyers recognize that the best agreement is a deal, not a steal that will antagonize the seller and the seller's agent.

Sellers need to competitively price their homes. Buyers need to know as much as possible about the property and any issues that may affect its suitability and desirability for them and to determine the price that they are willing to pay for that property.

When working with sellers and buyers, real estate licensees should repeatedly emphasize

sellers are required to disclose, and
buyers are obligated to discover.
The licensee's role differs depending whether the licensee is working with a member of the general public or for a client. Duties owed to any person, represented or not, include the following:

Present all offers in a timely manner.
Account for money or other property received.
Provide an explanation of the scope of services to be provided.
Be fair and honest and provide accurate information.
Provide limited confidentiality.
Disclose material adverse facts.
Duties are added when the member of the public decides to enter into a written agency agreement to act as an agent. An agent is required to offer client-level duties: obedience, loyalty, disclosure, confidentiality, accounting, and reasonable skill and care. Agents owe their clients the additional duties of advice, counsel, and assistance in negotiations.
