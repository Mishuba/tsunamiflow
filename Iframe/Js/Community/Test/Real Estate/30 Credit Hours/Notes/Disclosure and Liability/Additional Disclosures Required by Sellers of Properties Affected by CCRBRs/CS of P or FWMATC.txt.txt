Current Status of Property or Factors Which May Affect The Closing

Owners are obligated to disclose any factors that may affect the closing. Such factors include the following:

Owner occupied
Leased
Short sale
Foreclosure
Bankruptcy
Estate
Vacant (If so, how long has it been vacant?)
Other?
If the property is subject to covenants, conditions, restrictions, bylaws, rules, or is a condominium, an additional addendum is required.
