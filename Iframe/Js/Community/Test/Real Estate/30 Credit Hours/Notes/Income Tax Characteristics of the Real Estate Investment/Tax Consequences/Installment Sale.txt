An installment sale is any sale in which the taxpayer receives payments in more than one tax year. The installment method of reporting allows the taxpayer to claim as income only the amount actually received in each tax year. This method has some interesting limitations. However, it is safe to say, on most seller-financed transactions of less than $150,000, the taxpayer may report the gain as received. It should be noted that in addition to the cash received in the year of sale, the taxpayer will be required to pay taxes on accelerated depreciation and any mortgage relief in excess of his or her basis in the property. A taxpayer may elect out of the installment method and report the entire gain in the year of sale, but this election may only be made in the year of sale and is irrevocable.
