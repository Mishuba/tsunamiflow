There are two notable exceptions to the use of the Disclosure of Brokerage Relationships form. They are contained in SC Code of Laws §40-57-370(G). The form does not need to be provided and the requirements do not apply if the
transaction is regarding the rental or lease of property; or the 
communication from the licensee is a solicitation of business. 

It is useful to remember the three D's of the Disclosure of Real Estate Brokerage Relationships form:
Disclose (the types of available agency relationships)
Discuss (the advantages and disadvantages)
Determine (what representation the consumer wants or does not want)

Acknowledgment of the Disclosure form
Occurs at the time of delivery
In any subsequent agency agreement
In the sales contract

What to do when the Contact is not face-to-face, such as from the internet or email
Delivery of the form can be electronic as well
Not enough to just send
Apply the three D's: disclose, discuss, determine
