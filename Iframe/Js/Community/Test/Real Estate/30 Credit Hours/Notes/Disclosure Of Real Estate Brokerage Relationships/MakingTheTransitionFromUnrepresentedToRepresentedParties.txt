Licensee Duties To Sellers 
A seller becomes a client of a real estate brokerage firm by signing a formal listing agreement with the brokerage firm. For a seller to become a client, this agreement must be in writing, and must clearly establish the terms of the agreement and the obligations of both the seller and the brokerage firm which becomes the agent for the seller. 
SC Code of Laws §40-57-137(C) The statutory obligations that real estate professionals working with sellers owe to their clients are set forth in license law. The law requires that upon reaching a written agreement to provide brokerage services to a seller, a seller's agent shall:
perform the terms of the written brokerage agreement made with the seller;
promote the interest of the seller by performing agency duties which include: 
seeking a sale at the price and terms stated in the brokerage agreement or at a price and terms acceptable to the seller, except that the licensee is not obligated to seek additional offers to purchase unless the brokerage agreement provides otherwise while the property is subject to a contract of sale;
presenting in a timely manner all offers and counteroffers to and from the seller, even when the property is subject to a contract of sale;
disclosing to the seller all relevant facts concerning the transaction which are actually known to the licensee or, if acting in a reasonable manner, should have been known to the licensee, except as directed otherwise in this section;
advising the seller to obtain expert advice on matters that are beyond the expertise of the licensee;
accounting in a timely manner, as required by this chapter, for all money and property received in which the seller has or may have an interest;
exercise reasonable skill and care in discharging the licensee's agency duties;
comply with all provisions of this chapter and with any regulations adopted by the department;
comply with all applicable federal, state, or local laws, rules, regulations, and ordinances related to real estate brokerage, including laws which relate to fair housing and civil rights;
preserve confidential information provided by the seller during the course of and following the agency relationship that might have a negative impact on the seller's real estate activity unless:
SC Code of Laws §40-57-137(D) A licensee acting as a seller's agent may offer alternative properties to prospective buyers. A licensee acting as a seller's agent also may list for sale competing properties.
A buyer becomes a client of real estate brokerage firm by signing a formal buyer agency agreement with the brokerage firm. For a buyer to become a client, this agreement must be in writing and must clearly establish the terms of the agreement and the obligations of both the buyer and the brokerage firm which becomes the agent for the buyer.


Licensee Duties To buyers
The duties of the buyer agent follow in the law and are exactly the same except the duties are to the buyer client instead of the seller client.
