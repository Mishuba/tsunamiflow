The provisions of SC Code of Laws §40-57-350 (I)(2) provide a BIC and associated licensees in one office of a real estate brokerage firm may conduct business with a client of another office of the real estate firm as a customer or client without creating a dual agency relationship, so long as the branch offices each have a separate BIC and do not share the same associated licensees.
This rule has the effect of incentivizing firms to get a separate BIC for each office so that the firm can more often avoid the difficulties and limitations of dual and designated agency.
Designated agents are not limited by the firm's representation of the other client, but instead have a duty to promote the best interests of their clients, including negotiating the price. Just like dual agency, the client must give informed consent. The consent must be signed by the buyer before they write the offer, and by the seller before they sign the sales contract.
