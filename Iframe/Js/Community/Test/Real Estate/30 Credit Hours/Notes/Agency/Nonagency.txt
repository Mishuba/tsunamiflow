Some states (including South Carolina) permit a nonagency relationship between real estate professionals and the buying and selling public. In South Carolina, real estate professionals not serving in an agency capacity are called transaction brokers. In other states, real estate professionals might be referred to as transaction brokers, intermediaries, or facilitators. Their fiduciary responsibilities are focused on honesty, fairness, and accountability. The nonagent provides real estate services, sometimes called ministerial acts, without entering into an agency relationship.
