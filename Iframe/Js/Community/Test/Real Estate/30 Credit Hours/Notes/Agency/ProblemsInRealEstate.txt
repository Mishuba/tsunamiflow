Many buyers, sellers, and even real estate professionals still wrongly believe (or act as if they believe) that the agent who brings the buyer to a transaction only represents the buyer, when, in fact, the licensee acting in that capacity may be a subagent of the seller or even a nonagent. Before we cover the specifics of the different agency relationships, let's briefly look at some of the problems that can arise in real estate agency, the consequences of these actions, and some ways to reduce risk in this area.
